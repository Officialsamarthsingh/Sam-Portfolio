Landing Page A/B Testing

PythonPandasNumPyStatsmodelsMatplotlib

Analysed 294,478 user interaction records to evaluate a redesigned landing page, first validating experiment integrity by checking traffic split and removing mismatched assignments. Ran a Two-Proportion Z-Test and confidence interval analysis: control 50.3% vs treatment 49.7%, p-value > 0.05, CI containing zero — no statistically significant lift.

Recommendation
Hold off on the redesign. Re-test with a longer observation window or a more distinct UI variant before committing engineering resources to deployment.

Customer Churn Analysis

PythonPandasMatplotlibSeaborn

EDA on a telecom dataset revealed 26.5% overall churn, but the distribution is highly uneven. Month-to-month customers churned 1,655 cases vs just 48 on two-year contracts — a 34× difference. Senior citizens churned at 41.7% vs 23.6%. Recommendation: contract upgrade incentives and a dedicated senior retention programme.

Recommendation
Push month-to-month customers toward annual contracts with a retention incentive, and build a dedicated outreach flow for senior citizens given their 41.7% churn rate.
