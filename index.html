Customer Churn Analysis

EDA on a telecom dataset revealed 26.5% overall churn, but the distribution is highly uneven. Month-to-month customers churned 1,655 cases vs just 48 on two-year contracts — a 34× difference. Senior citizens churned at 41.7% vs 23.6%. Recommendation: contract upgrade incentives and a dedicated senior retention programme.
